# Xylem hackathon assessment and remaining work

Prepared November 13, 2026 Pacific · California v1 · UI build `efb5e6e0e986`

## Executive Summary

**Xylem is a credible working prototype with a plausible prize case, but the evidence does not support calling it a likely winner.** Its strengths are a concrete funding decision, inspectable allocations, geographic context, sensitivity controls and consistent exports. The weaker parts of the competition case are independent user evidence, demonstrated novelty and judge-ready delivery.

At $500M, the hosted desktop app and independently reconciled CSV agree on 97 systems and 476,663 modeled people. That establishes software consistency, not measured safe-water benefit. Annual-risk interpretation, population overlap and implementation costs remain unresolved. [1]

One newly confirmed defect appears in the funded plan: CA5610029 is named “10/30/2026.” The state identifies it as Vineyard Avenue Acres MWC. The private host also fails your retained exact-HTML requirement. [2][3]

**Prioritize a narrow credibility pass, practitioner feedback, a reliable demo and the submission story.** More model families, geographies or map layers would not address the principal gaps.

**Impact: meaningful potential, limited outcome evidence.** The software can expose small-system tradeoffs, but modeled populations and costs are proxies. No completed practitioner study, measured decision-time improvement or intervention benefit is established.

**Challenge fit and feasibility: strong alignment, incomplete operational proof.** California data and working allocations fit a real problem. Readiness, user adoption, project quotes, operating costs and a useful handoff to a planner still need evidence.

**Innovation: promising integration, uniqueness not established.** The defensible contribution is combining allocation, visible priorities, sensitivity and export. Knapsack optimization, prediction ensembles and maps are established methods. California already publishes risk, failing-system and affordability dashboards. Compare the task and output; do not claim these tools lack features without checking them. [5]

These are assessment judgments against the public criteria, not official scores or invented judging weights.

## What can honestly be said about winning

**A prize is plausible; first place is not yet a defensible expectation.** You have substantive working software to demonstrate. A polished map will not settle judges’ questions about actual use, originality and practical outcomes.

There is no reliable percentage estimate. Current competitors, the number and quality of eligible entries, your track and the final presentation are unknown. A number such as 70% would be fabricated.

The strongest improvement would be one intended user making one concrete decision, an observed reviewer objection and response, a clear difference from an existing workflow, reliable access and a realistic pilot plan. Without these, the entry risks looking like a well-built analytical dashboard whose practical value is still assumed. This is a competition-position judgment, not a statistical forecast.

## Confirmed issues and open risks

**1. Wrong system name — confirmed defect.** Exactly one of 1,062 saved app names is date-shaped. CA5610029 is selected in the default $500M plan, so the defect reaches its card and CSV. The official name is Vineyard Avenue Acres MWC. Trace the source and prepare a reviewed name-only correction or explicit ID fallback; the corruption’s original cause remains unresolved. Preserve frozen results rather than silently editing source data. [2][3]

**2. Hosted release — confirmed blocker under your requirement.** All 23 non-HTML files matched, but the host added a 1,114-byte script to the original HTML. Strict HTML bytes remain FAIL; you declined the exception. The private site also admits only your account, so judge access cannot be assumed. The prepared Pages route needs an existing repository admin/maintainer. [1]

**3. Scientific validity — known limitation.** Primary diagnostic AUC is 0.831 overall and 0.764 among At-Risk systems, with only 32 positive At-Risk outcomes. The diagnostic year was previously examined; publication timing and transfer of calibration to the final refit remain unresolved. The original 582-system comparison does not demonstrate ranking improvement. Annual and multi-year risk are unvalidated. Do not pitch these as proven annual probabilities or prevented failures. [6]

**4. Impact and baseline — interpretation risks.** Reach sums system populations without resolving overlap. Status-first is a simple comparator, not the state’s actual allocation process. Reserve mode uses sequential optimization, not a globally optimal reserve constraint. These are disclosed limitations but still contradict a pitch claiming unique beneficiaries, objectively best decisions or measured government outperformance. [6]

**5. Costs and routes — feasibility limits.** State averages and scaling are not project quotes. Routes are geographic approximations, with capacity, readiness, consent and operating costs unresolved. A route is a screening lead, not a buildable project. [6]

**6. Real-device accessibility — unverified.** Desktop keyboard, native Chrome 200% zoom and actual desktop download/open checks have passing evidence. Physical phones, native phone text settings, spoken VoiceOver/TalkBack and actual phone files remain unverified. Automated accessibility scans do not certify accessibility. The direct security-global probe was unavailable; successful app initialization supports an inference that its snapshot digest path completed. [1]

**7. Submission package — not verified.** No final deck, compliant video, team-complete project page or published submission was established in the supplied evidence. These may exist elsewhere. The saved five-minute walkthrough needs shortening for the required video. [7]

**8. Naming and differentiation — presentation risks.** The project shares the sponsor’s name. Give the independent entry a distinct title and explain its relationship to the event. This is a clarity recommendation, not a trademark-permission conclusion. Check third-party attribution and redistribution rights for submitted assets. No root LICENSE was found, though vendor notices are present; a project license is not established as a submission requirement. [2]

The full frozen-data scan found unique IDs throughout, no duplicate name/county search labels, no missing or zero coordinates, no missing At-Risk forecasts and no nonpositive populations/connections. It does not validate every source field or resolve population overlap. The identity defect does not invalidate the separately checked allocation arithmetic. [2]

## What belongs after the hackathon

Annual-risk validation requires original publication vintages, complete failure episodes including recovered systems, time-aligned features and genuinely independent evaluation. Another UI test or relabeling the existing diagnostic year cannot supply that evidence.

Real adoption needs project quotes, implementation constraints, overlap-aware benefit measures, user studies and prospective pilot results. A new region requires its own data/cost definitions and validation.

For this entry, make a smaller claim convincingly: the software helps users inspect a modeled funding tradeoff and export a consistent shortlist while keeping assumptions visible.
